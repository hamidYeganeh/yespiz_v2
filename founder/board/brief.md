# Brief · yespiz

Read `founder/idea.md` first. Vienna, Austria is the pilot. The restaurant is the seller and yespiz earns a commission. The customer chooses pizza through an admin-managed yespiz menu without browsing restaurant profiles. For the Austrian pilot, the proposed checkout reveals legally required seller details before the buyer commits. The founder requires admin-managed geography (country, region, city and neighbourhood) and editable commercial terms. Exact prices, rates, budget and buyer segment remain open.

The system ranks nearby eligible restaurants using internal quality score, active queue and other signals. Each offer gives a restaurant 45 seconds to accept or reject. Declines or expiry advance the offer to another restaurant. The customer sees an estimated delivery time only after acceptance, based on queue, the order and distance, then sees live progress.

Partners hand ready orders to their own pre-enrolled couriers. Couriers may work with multiple partners, and must check in at the current restaurant via QR or numeric code before pickup. Each trip holds one to three orders, and the courier app orders the stops. Couriers report incidents in-app and admin can intervene. There is no customer delivery fee. The founder requests cash, Stripe, Klarna, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
